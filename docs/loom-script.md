# Loom script: one report, intake to closure

**Before recording:** click **Reset demo**, switch to **Maria**, and open `/report` in a second tab.

---

## Open

> "Hi, thanks for having me.
>
> Your RFP asks for a centralized intake, reporting and case management system for employee relations across San Bernardino County. That work is spread across four teams: HR Business Partners, Central Investigations, EEO, and Return to Work. Together they support over 20,000 employees in more than 40 departments.
>
> Today, complaints come in by email, phone, in person and through a hotline. They're tracked in Microsoft Lists, Outlook and Teams. So the information is scattered, it's hard to track, it's hard to audit, and it can't take advantage of what newer technology can do.
>
> What I'll walk you through today is one user journey, start to finish:
>
> - First, an employee submits a complaint through our intake portal.
> - Then our EEO investigator, Maria, triages it and opens a case.
> - She works the case through to her findings.
> - Her manager, Angela, reviews and approves those findings.
> - Finally, Maria closes the case, and we'll see it show up on the dashboard.
>
> Two things to keep in mind along the way. Every AI suggestion is editable and needs a person's sign-off before it counts. And every action is recorded in an audit trail."

## 1. Intake · `/report`

> "Everything starts here, on our reporting page. There are three types of reports:
>
> - **Discrimination, harassment or retaliation.** That's EEO.
> - **A conduct or policy concern.** That's general employee relations.
> - **An accommodation request.** That goes to Return to Work.
>
> Each one has its own form."

Click **Discrimination, harassment or retaliation**.

> "I can submit anonymously. If I do, I get a private code so I can still check status and message the investigator. Today I'll submit with my name."

Fill in the form as you talk:
1. Name, email, "County employee"
2. Basis: **Sexual orientation**
3. What happened: "I was discriminated against on the basis of my sexual orientation by the head of Parks."
4. Date
   > "Only today or earlier. It won't take a future date."
5. Department: **Parks**
6. Location: **County headquarters**
7. People involved: head of Parks
8. Witness: director of HR

> "I can also upload files, or ask for an accommodation to take part in the process."

**Submit.**

> "My report is submitted, and I get a reference number I can use to follow its progress."

## 2. Triage · switch to **Maria**

> "Now I'll switch over to Maria's view. This is her home page: the cases she owns, where each one stands, and what she needs to do next. We're going to go to her intake queue.
>
> Here's the report I just submitted. When a report comes in, AI does a first pass. It summarizes the report, flags anything important, suggests a case type, and pulls likely respondents and witnesses from the HR system. Here it found Linda Park, the head of Parks, as a potential respondent, and Lisa Chen, the director of HR, as a witness.
>
> All of this is marked as an AI draft, and every field is editable, so Maria confirms it before anything happens."

Point at a field or change one. Change it back if needed.

> "I'll accept and create the case."

## 3. Working the case

> "This is the case view, and there are two main things to track.
>
> Along the top is the case status, from intake to closed, so I always know where I am and what's next.
>
> Along the bottom are the parts of the case. These are separate from the stages on purpose. A case keeps changing over time, and I need to be able to add to or edit any part of it at any stage, not just the stage I'm in."

Go through the tabs:

1. **Overview.**
   > "A summary of the case and the allegations."
2. **Parties.**
   > "Everyone involved: the head of Parks as respondent, and Lisa Chen as witness. I can add, edit or remove people. This connects to the HR system, so I can find anyone by name, employee ID or title."
3. **Allegations.**
   > "The allegations, and I can add more by hand."
4. **Interviews.**
   > "From here I can schedule interviews with respondents and witnesses, and keep my interview notes."
5. **AI panel** (on the side).
   > "Alongside the case, the AI can suggest next steps, summarize interview notes, or draft a first version of the report. Always as a draft for me to review."
6. **Documents.**
   > "Upload any evidence or related materials."
7. **Tasks.**
   > "These are the tasks the workflow created for me. Instead of tracking work across emails and lists, it's all in one place. I can add tasks or mark them done."
8. **Notes.**
   > "Internal notes for the case team, or external notes the reporter can see."

## 4. Findings

Open **Findings** → **Draft investigative report**.

> "Now I'll draft the investigative report. The AI builds a first version from my notes, and I edit it myself. It's always reviewed by a person. We're also adding a drafting module, so investigators can write the report from scratch without relying on AI at all."

Edit a line, click **Save**, then **Submit for approval**.

> "I can move the case forward stage by stage until I reach Findings. Once I'm there, I can't move forward on my own. My findings need my manager's approval."

## 5. Approval · switch to **Angela**

> "Now I'll switch to Angela, Maria's manager. She sees the same case: the interviews, the notes, every task on the case.
>
> She opens the findings report and sees it's pending approval. From here she can request changes from Maria, or approve."

Click **Approve findings**.

## 6. Close · switch back to **Maria**

> "Back in Maria's view, her findings are approved, so she can move to resolution and close the case. To close, she has to choose an outcome."

Choose **Sustained**, add the note "Corrective action taken," and close the case.

> "The case is closed, and the outcome is recorded. Because this is an EEO case, it was only visible to the EEO team the whole time."

Open the **Audit** tab.

> "And here's the audit trail I mentioned. Every view, edit, approval and AI use, with who did it and when. It can be exported."

## 7. Dashboard

> "On the dashboard, the closed case shows up right away, along with open cases and pending approvals."

## Close

> "So that's one complaint, from intake to closure, across two roles, in one system. AI does the busywork, people make the decisions, and everything is on the record.
>
> There's a lot more I didn't get to today: Return to Work tracking, reporting and exports, and the admin controls for turning AI features on and off. I'd love to walk you through any of it. Thanks for your time."
